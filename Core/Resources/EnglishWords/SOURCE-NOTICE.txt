ClearKeyboard English word recognition data

Ordinary dictionary headwords and source-listed inflections are derived from Alan Beale's
Alt12Dicts 2020.12.07, distributed by Kevin Atkinson in en-wl/wordlist.
Frozen upstream commit: 5ef55f9c42730ebe4394a78b77855468a6f15dd2
Upstream documentation: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/en-wl/wordlist/5ef55f9c42730ebe4394a78b77855468a6f15dd2/alt12dicts/README

The Alt12Dicts README states:
All of these files have been explicitly placed in the Public Domain by Alan Beale.

The 2of12id inflection revision additionally carries AGID/WordNet notices.
Those permissive notices are reproduced in AGID-LICENSE.txt in this directory.

ClearKeyboard's pre-existing hand-maintained common English seed words are included
as a small supplemental vocabulary; no pinyin-specific denylist or example-specific
allowlist has been added.

Extraction: lowercase ASCII headwords present in at least four source dictionaries;
capitalized names, abbreviations, multiword entries and annotated unusual forms are
excluded. Only unmarked source-listed inflections of admitted headwords are included.
Recognized input is never rewritten or case-folded in the displayed candidate.
